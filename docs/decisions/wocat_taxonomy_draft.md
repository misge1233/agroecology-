## §3.7 Land degradation types (verify codes)

landslides · Wr riverbank erosion · Wc coastal erosion · Wo offsite effects.
Wind erosion E: Et loss of topsoil · Ed deflation and deposition · Eo offsite effects.
Chemical C: Cn fertility decline and reduced SOM (not caused by erosion) · Ca acidification ·
Cp soil pollution · Cs salinization/alkalinization.
Physical P: Pc compaction · Pk slaking and crusting · Pi soil sealing · Pw waterlogging ·
Ps subsidence of organic soils · Pu loss of bio-productive function due to other activities.
Biological B: Bc reduction of vegetation cover · Bh loss of habitats · Bq quantity/biomass

decline · Bl loss of soil life · Bp increase in pests/diseases, loss of predators.

groundwater/aquifer level · Hp decline in surface water quality · Hq decline in groundwater
quality · Hw reduction in buffering capacity of wetlands.
